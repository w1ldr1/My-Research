## About Page Draft

### Hero

Headline:
- `Why Tools & Table exists`

Opening paragraph:
- `Tools & Table exists to help people cook real food with more confidence, better judgment, and less nonsense. The goal is not perfection. The goal is competence: understanding heat, salt, timing, texture, and the tools that actually make a difference.`

### Section: Where this comes from

Heading:
- `Where this comes from`

Paragraph:
- `This site comes from years of cooking, testing, feeding people, making mistakes, fixing them, and learning which traditions still matter. Some recipes come from old world foundations. Some come from modern American kitchens. The common thread is simple: the food has to be worth making again.`

Optional more personal paragraph:
- `Tools & Table grew out of the belief that too much food content talks past people. It either oversimplifies everything or performs expertise without teaching anything useful. This site is an attempt to do the opposite.`

### Section: What you will find here

Heading:
- `What you will find here`

Bullets:
- `Recipes that explain what matters and why`
- `Technique guides that make other recipes easier`
- `Tool recommendations based on actual kitchen use`
- `Honest advice for beginners and serious home cooks alike`

### Section: How we work

Heading:
- `How we work`

Paragraph:
- `Recipes are written to be cooked by real people in real kitchens. Tool recommendations are based on use, not hype. When something is difficult, we say so. When something is worth the money, we explain why. When it is not, we say that too.`

Optional disclosure paragraph:
- `Some tool links may eventually be affiliate links, but that will never change how a recommendation is made. If a product does not earn its place, it does not get recommended.`

### Section: Who this is for

Heading:
- `Who this is for`

Paragraph:
- `If you are just getting started, this site will help you build fundamentals that stick. If you already know your way around a stove, it will give you deeper techniques, better judgment, and stronger tools for thinking about food.`

### Closing CTA
