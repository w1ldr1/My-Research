MILITARY TAX PREPARATION WEBSITES - RESEARCH (March 7, 2026)
=============================================================

BEST OPTIONS - COMPLETELY FREE FOR MILITARY

1. MilTax (Military OneSource) - TOP RECOMMENDATION
   - Website: https://www.militaryonesource.mil/financial-legal/taxes/miltax-military-tax-services/
   - Federal: FREE
   - State: FREE (up to 3 returns)
   - Who Qualifies: All active duty, Guard, Reserve, and veterans
   - Notes: DoD-sponsored; built for military-specific situations (combat pay,
     deployments, multistate filings); no income limits, no rank restrictions

2. TurboTax Military Edition
   - Website: https://turbotax.intuit.com/personal-taxes/online/military-edition.jsp
   - Federal: FREE
   - State: FREE
   - Who Qualifies: Enlisted E-1 through E-9, active duty & Reserve
   - Notes: Does NOT cover officers or National Guard; excludes TurboTax Live products

3. TaxAct Military
   - Website: https://www.taxact.com/military
   - Federal: FREE
   - State: FREE
   - Who Qualifies: All active duty military (no rank restriction)
   - Notes: Enter Employer ID number during filing to claim offer

4. FreeTaxUSA Military
   - Website: https://www.freetaxusa.com/military/
   - Federal: FREE
   - State: $14.99
   - Who Qualifies: All military
   - Notes: Federal always free for everyone; cheapest paid state option

OTHER DISCOUNTED OPTIONS

- H&R Block: Available through IRS Free File program; 10% discount at select
  Exchange locations (Army & Air Force)
- Liberty Tax: Use coupon code MILITARY25 for discounted pricing (active duty & veterans)
- VITA Program: Free in-person tax assistance at many military installations

SUMMARY RECOMMENDATION
----------------------
Best overall: MilTax - 100% free federal + state, no rank/income restrictions,
              designed specifically for military tax situations.

Runner-up:    TaxAct Military - free federal + state for all active duty,
              no rank restrictions, commercial-grade interface.
