## Network Coverage Summary

- **T-Mobile:** Best 5G coverage by landmass (~49% US). Best for suburban/rural. Starlink T-Satellite most mature dead-zone solution.
- **Verizon:** Best urban performance. C-Band + mmWave. AST SpaceMobile satellite coming mid-2026.
- **AT&T:** Best rural reliability via FirstNet. Best for Americas travel. Excellent 4G LTE fallback.

## Key Gotchas

- **Deprioritization is real:** Base MVNO plans can drop below 1 Mbps at busy towers during peak hours
- **"Unlimited" data caps:** Most plans throttle after 35–100GB (to 128–256 Kbps — effectively unusable)
- **Annual prepay = no refunds:** Mint, US Mobile annual plans offer no refund if service disappoints
- **International coverage:** Most plans only cover Canada/Mexico. True 200+ country coverage: Google Fi Unlimited Plus, T-Mobile Experience plans, AT&T Premium 2.0 only
- **Device compatibility:** Carrier-locked phones may not work with all MVNOs, especially Verizon-based ones
